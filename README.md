# 📊 Adidas Sales Report – Power BI Project  

## 🔹 Project Overview  
This project provides an **interactive sales and profit analysis for Adidas** using Power BI. The dashboard highlights overall business performance by region, retailer, sales method, and product category. It enables data-driven decision-making by offering clear insights into revenue, operating profit, units sold, and pricing trends.  

## 🔹 Business Objective  
To analyze Adidas’s sales data and identify key drivers of revenue and profit, helping optimize product strategies, sales channels, and regional performance.  

## 🔹 Key Features  
- **KPIs:** Total Sales ($899.9M), Operating Profit ($332.1M), Units Sold (2M), Avg. Price per Unit (45.22).  
- **Sales Analysis:** By year, quarter, region, state, and city.  
- **Product Insights:** Men’s Street Footwear achieved the highest sales ($209M, 593K units sold).  
- **Channel Performance:** In-store sales outperformed online and outlet sales.  
- **Profitability by Retailer:** West Gear and Amazon contributed significantly.  
- **Regional Trends:** Northeast and West were top-performing regions with higher operating profits.  

## 🔹 Insights & Findings  
- **Q3 recorded the highest sales peak**, while Q1 was the lowest.  
- **In-store sales dominate**, indicating strong offline presence.  
- **Top-performing cities** include Charleston, New York, and Miami.  
- Strategic focus on **Men’s Footwear and high-profit regions** can further drive growth.  

## 🔹 Tools & Skills Utilised  
- **Power BI** (Data Modeling, DAX, Interactive Dashboards, Slicers & Filters)  
- **Data Analysis & Visualization**  
- **Business Intelligence & Reporting**  

## 🔹 How to Use This Project  
1. Clone or download this repository to your local system.  
2. Open the `Adidas_Sales_Report.pbix` file in **Power BI Desktop**.  
3. Explore the interactive dashboard using available slicers (State, City, Region, Sales Method).  
4. Review KPIs and visualizations to gain insights into Adidas sales performance.
